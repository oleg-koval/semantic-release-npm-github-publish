# olko-growth

Use a real, usable benefit or validated problem to design a measurable adoption experiment. Read current product behavior, audience evidence, positioning, and target instructions. Choose one audience, one job, one channel, a bounded test, a primary value metric, guardrails, and a stop or change threshold. Check that the proposed exposure reaches the intended audience and that the product can fulfill the promise during the test.

Separate acquisition, activation, retention, and revenue; define the event and time window behind each metric. Do not infer revenue from clicks or intent, or invent testimonials, counts, demand, urgency, or partnerships. Keep drafts editable. Publication, outreach, ads, and spending require explicit authorization. Return the hypothesis, evidence, experiment, measurement, risks, and next review point.

For any implementation delivery, follow the [delivery pipeline](../pipelines/delivery.md) before claiming completion.
